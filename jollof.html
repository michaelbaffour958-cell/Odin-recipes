Recipe For Jollof rice

Description

Jollof rice is a beloved West African dish made by cooking rice in a rich, flavorful tomato and pepper sauce. Popular across Ghana, Nigeria, and many other West African countries, it's a staple at parties, celebrations, and everyday family meals.
The dish gets its signature smoky depth from a blend of tomatoes, onions, and spices slow-cooked together before the rice is added, letting every grain soak up the bold flavors.

Every region and even every household has its own twist — some cooks add a touch of ginger or nutmeg, others swear by cooking it over an open fire for that extra smoky "party jollof" taste that's hard to recreate in a regular kitchen.
